# Gateway orbital environment

Source: [NASA Gateway Lunar Space Station](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/gateway-lunar-space-station/).
Original GLB: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/cds/3d/resources/model/gateway/Gateway%20Core.glb

Credit: NASA/JSC/Gateway Program Office. Alberto G. Bertolin and Bradley W. Reynolds, Graphics and Animation LAB, Johnson Space Center.

The complete connected station retains the original geometry, about 92,000 triangles. Draco mesh compression and WebP textures reduce the original 66 MB download to about 3 MB. Base-color maps use up to 1024 pixels; supporting maps use up to 512 pixels. Material brightness and the array tint are adjusted to keep the health interface readable.

This is an artistic orbital setting beside Earth, not Gateway’s lunar orbit or an operational flight configuration. The original station hierarchy remains connected. Limited arm joint motion in Devices is visual only: it does not operate hardware or imply a medical device connection. Slow astronaut and station drift honor reduced-motion preferences.

The station, astronaut and Earth remain genuine 3D geometry with continuous camera parallax. Station geometry stays in the scene across views; it is not switched off for Profile or Urgent response.
